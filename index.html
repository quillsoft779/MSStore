The shutter doesn't come down.

Most hardware shops close at nine. Pipes don't burst on schedule and breakers don't trip by appointment — so we stay open, all day and all night, every day of the year.

  1. 23:40 A storm trips the main breaker — and the spare is the wrong rating.
  2. 02:15 A tank connector gives out and water is on the floor.
  3. 05:30 The site starts at six and the plumber is two elbows short.

Whatever the hour, call ahead and we'll have it on the counter when you arrive.
