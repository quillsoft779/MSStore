logo

A Pakistani 3-pin socket where the big earth pin on top is a drop of water: electric and sanitary in one simple shape, set on a rounded plate like a switch plate. It reads at any size, from a WhatsApp photo to a shop signboard.

  1. 01The plate. The rounded square is a switch plate, the thing every customer installs. It also works as an app icon and a profile photo.
  2. 02The two pins. The live and neutral holes at the bottom, laid out like the 3-pin socket in every Pakistani home. They stand for the electric side of the shop.
  3. 03The drop. The big earth pin on top becomes a water drop, for sanitary and plumbing. It's in safety yellow, the colour of the signboard.

Using the logo

Do
  • Leave empty space around the logo, at least the width of one socket hole.
  • Use the yellow version on dark or busy backgrounds.
  • Keep the drop yellow on the ink version.
Don't
  • Stretch, tilt or add shadows and outlines.
  • Recolour it outside the four colours above.
  • Put the ink version on a dark photo.
Typefaces: Archivo (condensed, 800) for the name · IBM Plex Mono for the trade line · Noto Nastaliq Urdu for the Urdu. All are free Google Fonts.
